# FAQ

## Why was my loan application rejected?
Common reasons may include a weaker credit score, an unfavorable loan term, lower income strength relative to the requested loan, or weaker overall financial support from declared assets.

## Why was my loan application approved?
Applications are more likely to be approved when the applicant shows strong creditworthiness, stronger income, and a healthier financial profile supported by assets.

## What factors matter most in this model?
The most important factors are credit score, loan term, loan amount, annual income, and declared asset values.

## Does credit score matter a lot?
Yes. Credit score is the strongest driver in the model and has a major influence on approval likelihood.

## Do assets matter?
Yes. Residential, commercial, and bank asset values all contribute to the financial strength of an application.

## Does education matter?
Education has some influence, but it is much less important than financial and credit-related features.

## What can improve approval chances?
Improving credit score, strengthening income proof, improving financial asset position, and keeping repayment burden reasonable may improve approval chances.
