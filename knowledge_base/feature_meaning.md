# Feature Meaning

- **no_of_dependents**: Number of people financially dependent on the applicant.
- **income_annum**: Annual income declared by the applicant.
- **loan_amount**: Amount of loan requested by the applicant.
- **loan_term**: Duration of the loan repayment period.
- **cibil_score**: Creditworthiness score of the applicant. Higher values generally indicate stronger credit history.
- **residential_assets_value**: Estimated value of residential assets owned by the applicant.
- **commercial_assets_value**: Estimated value of commercial assets owned by the applicant.
- **luxury_assets_value**: Estimated value of luxury assets declared by the applicant.
- **bank_asset_value**: Estimated value of bank-held assets declared by the applicant.
- **education**: Education category of the applicant.
- **self_employed**: Whether the applicant is self-employed or not.
