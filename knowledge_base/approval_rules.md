# Approval Rules

## Main approval tendencies observed from the model

- A higher **cibil_score** strongly improves the likelihood of loan approval.
- Higher declared **income_annum** generally supports approval, although its effect should be interpreted together with other financial variables.
- Higher asset values, especially **residential_assets_value**, **commercial_assets_value**, and **bank_asset_value**, tend to strengthen the application.
- The relationship between **loan_amount** and other financial indicators is important. Loan amount should not be interpreted alone.
- A longer **loan_term** can negatively affect approval likelihood in many cases.
- A higher **no_of_dependents** may slightly weaken the application when repayment burden is a concern.
- **Education** has some effect, but it appears weaker than major financial and credit-related variables.
- Financial and credit-related variables are more influential than personal background variables.

## Most influential features overall

1. cibil_score  
2. loan_term  
3. loan_amount  
4. income_annum  
5. residential_assets_value  
6. commercial_assets_value  
7. bank_asset_value  
8. luxury_assets_value
